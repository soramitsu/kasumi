5. Channels, reviews, advisories and fixes5. 窓口・レビュー・セキュリティ情報・修正

Support is provided in Japanese and English through email and the support portal, plus the channels, reviews and named Soramitsu staff listed for your plan on the pricing page. Security advisories are published for all users; Business adds direct notice at publication, and Enterprise adds advance mitigation notice to an enrolled security contact when disclosure conditions permit, without a fixed notice period. Fixes ship in published maintenance releases. For confirmed Sev 1–2 defects, Business includes handover to the development team and consideration of a backport to a supported branch, and Enterprise includes priority hotfix assessment on the covered build with rollback instructions. Bespoke branches and extended maintenance need a separate order.

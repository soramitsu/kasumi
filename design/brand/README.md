# KASUMI logo

Final artwork: kasumi-logo-with-katakana.svg. Native vector paths, transparent background; katakana is outlined in this export. The Figma original keeps カスミ as editable Noto Sans JP Medium text.

Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/e3nD9v8xlnjyBevEnXKike/logo-brand?node-id=359-3
Page: KASUMI · Logo

Red: #E3232C. Five stylized shide hang from the rope. The visible カスミ glyphs span exactly from the left edge of M to the right edge of I.

Image concept generated with the built-in image_gen tool, then traced to Bézier paths with Potrace and assembled in Figma. kasumi-logo-shide.png is the five-shide image reference before the katakana addition. kasumi-logo.svg is the same pre-katakana vector; use kasumi-logo-with-katakana.svg for the completed design.

## Final image prompts

Five-shide edit:

Make one tiny precise edit to this logo: change the FOUR hanging red zigzag shide paper streamers on the curved connecting rope to exactly FIVE. There must be exactly 5 individual, clearly separated, downward-hanging red zigzag streamers, evenly spaced along the rope between the two rocks. Retain their existing Japanese folded-paper zigzag shape, making them slightly narrower if needed for five to fit gracefully with white gaps. Preserve the rock shapes, mist bands, rope curve, all proportions and layout, KASUMI typography and spacing, and all other content. Crucial: flat SOLID RED #E3232C logo on PURE WHITE #FFFFFF background, absolutely NO black, glow, gradients, shadows, halos or texture. This is crisp two-color vector-style artwork. Count FIVE shide. Only change the number of shide from four to five; leave the rest unchanged.

Flat-color refinement:

Restore this image to a flat red logo on PURE WHITE. Preserve the exact shapes, positions, KASUMI wordmark and all FIVE hanging zigzag shide streamers. Change rendering only. Every background pixel must be pure white #FFFFFF; every logo shape must be flat opaque red #E3232C. NO BLACK, NO GLOW, no lighting, gradients, halos, shadows, texture, outline or transparency. The entire canvas should be a bright WHITE background with a crisp solid RED logo. Keep exactly FIVE separate shide under the rope. Flat red vector-style logo on white.

Final typography added natively in Figma: カスミ, Noto Sans JP Medium, 48 px, 69.699524 px tracking, 64 px line height, red #E3232C. Visible glyph edges align at x=1063 (left of M) and x=1333.055054 (right of I), verified using actual rendered glyph bounds rather than the text box. Text position: x=1058.296021, y=909.
